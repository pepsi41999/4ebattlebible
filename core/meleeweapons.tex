\chapter{Melee Weapons}

\section{Overview}
All melee weapons have Characteristics that define how
powerful they are and how to use them.

\section{Melee Weapon Characteristics}

\weapon{power fist}{-}{x2}{2}{Melee}

\section{The Characteristics}

\subsection{Range}

All melee weapons are limited to Close Combat.
\subsection{Strength (S)}

Uses the model’s S.
\subsection{Armor Piercing Value (AP)}

The lower the better. See page XX for rules on Saving Throws
and AP.
\subsection{Weapon (Type)}

Describes special rules particular to the weapon.

\section{Melee Weapon Type}
The Melee Weapon Types available and their effects are listed
below.


\subsection{Close Combat Weapon (CCW)}
Includes chainswords, axes, pistols, etc. Weapons of this type
confer no bonus to S when used during the Assault Phase but
do grant +1A during the Assault Phase. If a Plasma Pistol is
used as a CCW it confers no bonus to S nor Armor Penetration
advantages.

\subsection{Heavy Close Combat Weapon}
The maximum Sv of any target against this weapon is 4+,
even if it would normally
 be better.
\subsection{Power Weapon}
Ignores the target Sv (but not Invulnerable Sv). When
specified, grants a bonus to S or Wounds on a fixed value
regardless of target T.

\subsection{Lightning Claws}
Ignores the target Sv (but not Invulnerable Sv), and may re-roll
any failed To-Wound rolls once. For a single model equipped
with two, +1A.

\subsection{Force}
Rolls To-Hit and To-Wound as normal, allowing any
Invulnerable Sv. For any Wound inflicted (failed Sv), the model
equipped with the Force Weapon makes a Psychic Test (see
page XX). On success the target suffers Instant Death,
regardless of remaining Wounds. Has no effect against targets
without Wounds (Vehicles).

\subsection{Power Fist/Claw}
Doubles user’s S (up to a maximum of S10), and hits ignore
Sv (but not Invulnerable Sv). Models attacking with this
weapon are always delivered at I1 and ignore any bonuses for
special rules/cover/etc.

\subsection{Thunder Hammer}
This weapon counts as a Power Fist, but also causes any
target Wounded but not killed to attack at Initiative 1 during the
next Assault Phase. Vehicles struck always suffer the “Crew
Shaken” effect in addit
ion to any other results.
\subsection{Witchblade}

Attacks with this weapon always make their To-Wound roll
against a fixed value of 2+. Armor Saves are made as normal.
Against Vehicles only, additionally multiplies S*3, up to a
maximum of S10.

\subsection{Rending}
All rolls To-Hit of 6 with this weapon results in an automatic
Wound that ignores Sv (but not Invulnerable Sv). Against
Vehicles only, roll an additional D6 if a result of 6 is obtained
when testing Armor Penetration, summing the result of both
rolls for the purposes of 
the test.
\subsection{Poisoned (X+)}
Hits from this weapon always wound on (X+), regardless of
attacker S or target T.
