\chapter{Unit Types}
\section{Overview}
This section provides the full rules for all of the Non-vehicle
model types listed on page 4. The special rules for the Unit
Type are broken down by Phase.

\section{Non Vehicles}
All Unit Types described here are considered Non Vehicle.
\section{Infantry}
The standard Unit Type. All Core rules apply to them as
written.
\section{Bikes}
\subsection{Movement}
Movement range is increased to 12”, and not slowed by
Difficult Terrain. However, they treat all Difficult Terrain a being
Dangerous. All Bikes have the Turbo Boosters special rule.
\subsection{Shooting}
May fire one weapon per rider, not per bike. Mounted Rapid
Fire weapons may fire once up to max range even if the Unit
has moved. Rapid Fire and Heavy weapons may be fired even
if the Unit moves, and the Bike may still Charge.
\subsection{Assault}
Charges have the same considerations as movement in the
Movement Phase (see above).
\subsection{Morale}
Falling back moves are 3D6”.
\subsection{Additional Protection}
Bikes increase their rider's Toughness by 1, but not for the
purpose of adjudicating Instant Death.
\section{Jetbikes}
\subsection{Movement}
Movement range is increased to 12”, and may move over any
other models. When moving over Difficult Terrain may choose
to not treat it as Dangerous Terrain, but doing so means they
may not use said Terrain for a Cover Save, and will be as tall
the Area Terrain for LOS purposes. All Jetbikes have the Turbo
Boosters special rule.
\subsection{Shooting, Assault, Morale, Additional Protection}
As Bikes, but Jetbikes cannot choose to move over Difficult
Terrain in the Assault Phase.
\section{Monstrous Creatures}
\subsection{Movement}
May re-roll Difficult Terrain Tests.
\subsection{Shooting}
Automatically passes Leadership Tests to target Units other
than the closest. May fire up to two weapons per turn.
\subsection{Assault}
All Wounds by Monstrous Creatures ignore Armor Saves (but
not Invulnerable Saves). When attacking a Vehicle in Close
Combat roll 2D6+Strength for Armor Penetration unless
specified otherwise.

\section{Jump Infantry}
\subsection{Movement}
When they are not using their Jump Packs, move as Infantry.
Otherwise they can move up to 12", aren't slowed by Difficult
Terrain and can move freely over other models or obstacles.
However, if they end their move in difficult terrain, they must
take a Dangerous Terrain test. If equipped with Jet Packs
reduce this range from 12” to 6”.
\subsection{Shooting}
If equipped with Jet Packs they can fire Rapid Fire weapons to
max Range even if they moved, and they may declare a
Charge even after firing their Rapid Fire weapons.
\subsection{Assault}
If equipped with Jet Packs they are allowed to move 6” in any
direction during Assault Phase even if not Charging.
\subsection{Morale}
Always Falls back 3D6”, compulsory use of Jump Packs.
Same considerations for any other Movement using Jump
Packs.
\section{Artillery}
\subsection{Movement}
Gun models must always take a Dangerous Terrain test while
moving through Difficult Terrain. Gun models without crew
may not move.
\subsection{Shooting}
A different Crewman must remain in Coherency with each gun.
Crewman may not fire other weapons so long as the gun
remains. Ranges are measured from the gun model. Unless
firing Barrage weapons, the target must be within line of sigh
of both the Gun and a Crewman in Coherency with it. When hit
by a non-Template or Blast weapon, roll 1D6: on a 1-4 the
Crew is hit, on a 5-6 the Gun is hit.
\subsection{Assault}
So long as the gun remains, not permitted to make a Charge.
Only the Engaged crew models fight in an Assault. If only the
Gun is engaged, hit rolls against it automatically succeed. If
both the Crew and the Gun are engaged, the enemy rolls to hit
against the Crew's WS, then randomize the hits as with
Shooting.
\subsection{Morale}
As Infantry, except if the unit does not have at least 1 crewman
per gun when making a Fall Back move the gun is destroyed,
and if forced to Fall Back any Sweeping Advance
automatically massacres the Artillery Unit.

\section{Beasts \& Cavalry}
\subsection{Movement}
All use the Universal Special Rule: Fleet (see page \pageref{sec:universalspecialrules}).
Otherwise, as Infantry.
\subsection{Assault}
May make 12” charges. When charging through Difficult
Terrain, make a regular Difficult Terrain test and double the
result.
\subsection{Morale}
Fall Back moves are 3D6”.
